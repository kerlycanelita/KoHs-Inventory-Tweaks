## Fixes

### A click could fire when the inventory closed

An attack or use pressed in an earlier input poll of the same tick as the
inventory key stayed queued under the early-opened inventory and fired when the
inventory closed: a ghost hit, or a thrown pearl. Vanilla runs those clicks in
that tick, so an opening with one pending now waits for the tick, as Vanilla's
own opening does.

### 26.2 handled some keys after the inventory

1.0.13 gave 26.2 the key order of 26.1.2. On 26.2, chat, commands,
advancements, social and the HUD toggle are handled before the inventory, so
pressed together with the inventory key they waited until it closed. Each
version now follows the order read from its own client jar.

### Shortcuts after a fast flick in containers

Minecraft updates the slot under the pointer when it draws the screen, so F,
1-9, Q, Ctrl+Q, a click or the wheel used right after a fast flick acted on the
slot the pointer had just left. The immediate targeting that already fixed this
in the player inventory now covers every Vanilla container: chests, barrels,
shulker boxes, hoppers, dispensers, crafting tables and crafters, furnaces,
brewing stands, enchanting tables, anvils, grindstones, stonecutters, looms,
cartography and smithing tables, beacons, villagers, horses and nautiluses.

### Rewritten opening logic

Super Fast Inventory now reaches one decision per input poll, from everything
that poll delivered, and follows each version's own key order. Center Mouse Fix
and Cursor Landing were split into screen classification, landing position and
the opening itself, so each part can be checked on its own.

## A new interface

- **Look.** Dark violet glass with cut corners, a slow background (a turning
  slot-grid sigil, embers and a passing light), section titles over blades and
  lines of current. Cyan marks speed and precision, magenta warnings and
  crimson danger. With Reduce inventory visual motion on, the background holds
  still; hover and click feedback remain.
- **Controls that never move.** Buttons, tabs and screens no longer grow into
  place when they open, and cards no longer lift on hover. The opening scale
  drew each control a few pixels away from where it could be clicked during the
  first third of a second; the openings are now light laid over controls that
  stay put.
- **Switches.** A track and a knob beside the state name, so the state reads by
  shape and by word.
- **Icons.** New pixel icons for every card, tab and compatibility state, sharp
  at every GUI scale.
- **Sharper previews.** The inventory previews are drawn at whole-pixel scales,
  and up to twice their size on the main menu and in Cursor Landing, where a
  larger preview makes a position easier to place.
- **Text that fits.** Card titles wrap between words only, the side columns take
  the width their longest word needs, and a label that cannot fit is shortened
  instead of spilling out. Every page fits at GUI scale 2, 3 and 4.
- **Warnings.** One shared panel with a slow magenta pulse that never flashes.

## Vanilla and network boundary

Opening and closing stay on Vanilla's paths. The release adds no packet, replays
no key and changes no server transaction, cooldown or mouse sensitivity.
Server-controlled screens keep their Vanilla timing.

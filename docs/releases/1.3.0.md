# KoHs Inventory Tweaks 1.3.0

Vanilla's inventory bugs fixed, a steadier inventory on servers with ping, faster
enchanted items, and an option to hide the recipe book.

## Fixed, always on

These are bugs in Minecraft itself. They are fixed on every version, with no switch.

- **Double clicks.** Since the double click moved into the mouse handler, a
  double click is remembered after it has done its work. A third quick click
  collected the stack again instead of placing it (MC-306262); placing a stack
  and shift-clicking it moved every matching stack (MC-301852); a drag started
  by the second click of a double click collected instead of spreading
  (MC-312333); and a double click released over another slot collected there
  (MC-311852). Each click now starts fresh after a collect or after a release
  that empties the cursor, as it did up to 1.21.8.
- **A held swap key swaps once.** Holding F or a number key over a slot made
  the key repeat swap it back and forth about thirty times a second, so the item
  ended up wherever the key happened to come up. The repeats now do nothing. The
  drop key still repeats on purpose.
- **Inventory and drop keys on mouse buttons.** Bound to a mouse button, the
  inventory key never closed a chest and the drop key never dropped from one,
  while the swap and number keys always worked (MC-577). They now act as their
  keyboard bindings do.
- **A smooth player in the inventory.** The inventory drew the player as of the
  end of the current tick, so its walk, swing and idle motion moved twenty times
  a second whatever the frame rate (MC-265935). It now uses the frame's moment,
  as the world does.
- **Toggled sprint and sneak on a mouse button come back** after an inventory
  or any screen closes, as they do on the keyboard (MC-308595).
- **Center Mouse Fix in every container.** It now centers the pointer in
  chests, shulker boxes, furnaces and every other screen opened from the game,
  not only in the inventory. After the window changed size, Vanilla opened a
  chest with the pointer 144 pixels from the center in the lab; with the fix, 0.

## New options

- **Steady inventory** (on). On a server with ping, the server answers almost
  every click with the whole inventory, because something else changed it while
  the click was travelling (armor losing durability in a fight is enough). When
  a second click follows within the ping, the answer to the first arrives after
  the second was already shown, and Vanilla flashes that slot back until its own
  answer arrives. Players see a totem or an item jump back, and pressing again
  undoes the first press. With Steady inventory, the slots changed by clicks
  still on their way keep showing those clicks; every other slot, a totem that
  pops, worn armor, an item picked up, shows at once. The server's last word is
  what stays, and the moment the inventory closes, it is all that is left. Nothing
  sent to the server changes. On a local Grim server with the armor wearing down,
  the double re-totem flickered in 4, 6 and 11 of 24 tries at 70, 130 and 200 ms
  of ping; with Steady inventory, in none, and Grim raised no alert.
- **Faster enchanted items** (on). Minecraft redraws every enchanted or animated
  item in menus and the hotbar from scratch on every frame (MC-310555). They are
  now redrawn sixty times a second, which looks the same: the shine moves less
  than a pixel between two redraws. In the lab, with a crystal PvP kit, the
  hotbar view ran about a third faster; the gain depends on what else limits
  your frame rate.
- **Hide recipe book** (off). Removes the green recipe book button and keeps the
  book shut in the inventory, the crafting table and the furnaces, so a stray
  click can never open it and push the inventory aside mid-fight. Your saved
  book setting is left as it was and nothing is sent to the server.
- **Leave with movement keys** (off). A movement key pressed in your inventory
  or a container closes it and starts the move in the same press. The close is
  sent before the movement, as when you close and then walk. Text boxes keep
  their keys.

## Super Fast Inventory and ping

The question was whether Super Fast Inventory still matters at 69 to 199 ms of
ping. Measured in the `sfi-value` lab:

- The inventory appears about 4 ms after the key instead of 21 to 31 ms (up to
  47 ms) with Vanilla.
- A hand that flicks to a totem and presses F within 40 ms of the inventory key
  gets the totem every time; with Vanilla, between none and 9 of 10 times,
  because the inventory is not there yet and the press goes to the world.
- The click never leaves sooner than a Vanilla client could send it. Ping adds
  the same delay to both, so Super Fast Inventory cannot make a click reach the
  server earlier, and nothing in this mod tries to.

What players at that ping actually see is the flicker that Steady inventory
removes.

## How it was checked

Every lab now runs in one game per version (`-PdebugMacro=suite`, about two
minutes, with `-PlabQuick`): the new `inventory-feel` lab plays each bug through
the game's own input handlers and checks the result in the inventory and on the
integrated server, next to `slot-shortcuts`, `inventory-order`, `close-hotbar`,
`cursor-contract`, `pvp-input`, `features` and `sfi-value`. Played against 1.2.1,
`inventory-feel` reproduces ten of the Vanilla bugs above; 1.3.0 passes all of
its checks on the five versions. The ping figures come from a local 26.2 server
with Grim 2.3.74 and Ping Equalizer. The research behind the release is in
`docs/audits/1.3.0-research-2026-10-18.md`.

## Fixes

### The highlighted slot and the slot that takes the click

Minecraft cuts the pointer to whole GUI pixels before it draws a screen, and
every slot edge sits on a whole pixel, so the slot it highlights is always the
slot a click at that pointer takes. The GUI Scaler converted that cut pixel to
the scaled inventory and rounded it, which near every slot edge highlighted one
slot while the click, F, 1-9 or Q went to its neighbour. It now converts the
exact pointer and cuts afterwards, as Minecraft does.

The `hover-agreement` lab walks the pointer across every column and row edge of
the player inventory a screen pixel at a time and compares the highlight each
frame drew with the slot a click there would take: 63 disagreements in 560 on
1.1.1, none on 1.2.0, on 26.2, 26.1.2, 26.1.1, 26.1 and 1.21.11.

### Center Mouse Fix in one move

GLFW puts the pointer back where it saved it when the mouse was captured as it
leaves disabled mode, after Minecraft has asked for the centre. Center Mouse Fix
used to read the pointer back afterwards and correct it. It now leaves disabled
mode first, so the request Minecraft makes moves the visible pointer, and nothing
reads or rewrites the pointer once the inventory exists. On Wayland, which
cannot place a free pointer, the release stays as in Vanilla.

### Player Visibility with a transparent backdrop

With the inventory backdrop at 0% there is nothing to reveal, so the
line-of-sight checks for the reveal windows no longer run. With a backdrop they
stop as soon as the nearest visible players are found.

## The Inventory Tweaks page, as a tree

Super Fast Inventory, Center Mouse Fix and Reduce inventory visual motion stay
where they were. Under Super Fast Inventory sits its one sub-option; the mascot
has its card at the end. Nothing here clicks, moves or sends anything for the
player.

| Option | Default | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shortcuts follow the pointer | On | Under Super Fast Inventory. Number keys, F and Q act on the slot under the pointer the moment they are pressed. Off, or with its parent off: the slot highlighted in the last frame, as in Vanilla. |
| Zymekoh mascot | On | The cat in every window. |

**Pixel-perfect scale** (on) moved to the GUI Scaler page, beside Affect
Containers: the scale uses whole screen pixels per inventory pixel, so slots
stay even and sharp.

## Animated icons and the Zymekoh mascot

The icons of every tab, option card and warning are now small scenes made of
Minecraft items and particle sprites on a transparent ground, animated and
woken up by the pointer: a slot with a totem in speed streaks for Inventory
Tweaks, a compass for Cursor Landing, a barrier for the Issues Tracker, dyes
that change colour for Customization, a spyglass for the GUI Scaler, an
emerald, a redstone torch or a barrier for the three compatibility levels.
They run on their own clock with their own sprites, so no graphics, particle,
glint or motion setting stills them.

A small cat made from the Zymekoh skin, both layers of it, lives on the free
floor of every window. It reacts to the switches and warnings of the window it
is in, follows the pointer, can be petted, carried, dropped or thrown: it
swings on the pointer, falls, tumbles, bounces off the edges of the window and,
thrown hard, splats against them with a cry. Its menu feeds it Totems of
Undying pulled by magic from the inventory preview; five make it heavy for ten
minutes. Eaten totems only fade in the preview, never in the real inventory.
It meows, purrs and eats with Minecraft's own sounds, which its menu turns off.

A new KoHs card under Behavior opens the KoHs tab of KoHs Anchor's and Crystal
Tweaks: Zymekoh, who makes the KoHs mods, as the KoHs Mod Suite site draws her,
with links to Discord, the site, Modrinth and Buy me a coffee. Bring the mascot to her face and
she gets cross, adorably.

## Verification

Run in the game on 26.2, 26.1.2, 26.1.1, 26.1 and 1.21.11:

- `hover-agreement` lab: 560 slot-edge positions at five GUI Scaler sizes, no
  disagreement on any version.
- `features` lab: pixel-perfect sizes at GUI scale 2, 3 and 4, the shortcut
  target with and without Super Fast Inventory, the page as a tree whose
  sub-option sleeps with its parent, the GUI Scaler switch, and the KoHs tab,
  which opens from its card, draws Zymekoh and makes her cross while the mascot
  is carried to her face. 63 checks, no failures.
- `mascot` lab on 26.1.2, 26.2 and 1.21.11: petting, the menu, five meals from
  the preview, the heavy drag, digestion, the disgust without totems, a drop,
  a splat and a bounce, recorded through the game's own input handlers.

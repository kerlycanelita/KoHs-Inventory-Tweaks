# KoHs Inventory Tweaks 1.4.0

Next Gen KoHs Inventories, an interface that follows your inventory's look, new hotbar
types, Russian, and Minecraft 26.3.

## Tabs, tooltips and buttons

A new page in Customization carries your inventory's look to the parts around it.

- The creative tabs no longer stay Vanilla grey beside a customized inventory.
  **Match all** gives them the inventory's colour and background image, **Match
  colour** only its colour, **Custom** a colour and opacity of your own, and
  **Vanilla** leaves them alone. Switches choose what follows: creative tabs, recipe
  book tabs, scrollbars with Fabric's page arrows, and effect panels.
- **Item tooltips** (on): the box and frame follow a customized inventory or your own
  colours. An item with its own tooltip style keeps it, and text colours stay as they
  are, because they tell rarity and enchantments apart.
- **Menu buttons** (off): colours Minecraft's own buttons in every menu.
- Container titles turn light by themselves on a dark panel.

## Next Gen KoHs Inventories

A new page, behind a warning: these inventories move your slots, some servers may ban
that, and Cursor Landing and mods that center the mouse may not work with them. The
page shows the inventory in the middle and the style selector below it. Every style
stays around the middle of the screen and has its own version in the Creative
inventory.

| Style | Looks like | Recipe book |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Broken** | Split in half, each half pulled aside with its slots | No |
| **Visor** | Only the slots, floating over the world | No |
| **Vertical** | Tall columns of slots beside the player and crafting | Yes |
| **Compact** | The smallest panel, without the player model | Yes |
| **Ring** | Two rings of slots around the player | No |
| **Honeycomb** | Staggered hexagon slots | Yes |
| **Fan** | Rows opening like a fan, with no panel behind them | No |
| **Hotbar Up** | The hotbar above the inventory | Yes |
| **Mirror** | Crafting on the left, the player and armor on the right | Yes |
| **Flipped** | The inventory on top, the player and crafting below | Yes |
| **Wide** | The player beside the inventory | No |
| **Side Hotbar** | The hotbar as a column | Yes |
| **Custom** | Wherever you put each slot | No |

**Custom** opens an editor: drag the hotbar as one piece and the other slots one by
one, or hold Shift for the opposite. The player model and the status effects can be
placed too; the effects are simulated in the editor, so they can be placed when none
is active.

The Visor draws only the outline of each slot, so the world stays in full view.

Styles with a recipe book open it beside the panel, as Vanilla does; the others keep it
shut. Only the screen changes: a slot keeps its index, so the server gets what a
Vanilla inventory sends for the same clicks.

## Hotbar

The same page changes the hotbar on the HUD:

- **Hotbar** type: Vanilla, Visor (a curved row of floating slots), Ghost (plain
  outlines), Split or Vertical.
- **Movement** (off): the hotbar leans and sways as you move, turn and fall.
- **Reactions** (off): it answers a popped totem, hearts lost or healed, low health,
  and hits taken or landed.
- **Bars** (on): health, armor, food and the XP bar move with the hotbar while Movement
  or Reactions is on. A mod that also moves these elements may cause crashes or
  instability.

## Also new

- **Russian**, and pre-reform Russian written from it.
- **Minecraft 26.3.** It moved from GLFW to SDL, so on 26.3 the background and
  configuration pickers open the system file dialog through SDL.
- Status effect panels fit the room they really have: with the GUI Scaler or a wide
  style they shrink to icons instead of running out of the window.
